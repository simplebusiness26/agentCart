# AgentCart go-to-market plan

## Positioning

Do not lead with "agentic commerce attribution". Lead with the merchant outcome:

> **How much money is AI sending your store?**

Secondary hook:

> **Is AI recommending your competitors instead of you?**

## Free acquisition product

The public readiness scanner is the top-of-funnel product.

Merchant enters a store URL and receives:

- AI shopping readiness score
- structured product-data check
- pricing/availability signals
- crawler accessibility
- machine guidance / sitemap checks
- concrete fixes

The free scan ends with the next action: connect Shopify to measure real identifiable AI referrals and revenue.

## Initial customer profile

Start narrowly with small-to-medium Shopify stores where:

- product catalogues are public
- online sales matter directly
- the owner can install apps without enterprise procurement
- merchandising/product data quality materially affects discovery

Avoid trying to sell every ecommerce platform on day one.

## First 50-store launch

1. Find 50 suitable Shopify stores.
2. Run the public AgentCart readiness scan on each store.
3. Keep only reports with real, specific findings.
4. Contact the merchant with the result rather than a generic pitch.
5. Offer free early access to attribution in exchange for feedback and permission to use anonymized aggregate findings.

Example outreach angle:

> We ran an AI-shopping readiness check on your store and found several product-data issues that can make it harder for AI shopping assistants to interpret your catalogue. The report is free. We're also testing a Shopify app that measures identifiable AI-referred visits and orders.

Do not claim a merchant is "losing £X" unless AgentCart has defensible data for that statement.

## Content engine

Use aggregated, anonymized product data to publish original findings rather than generic AI posts.

Examples:

- "We scanned 500 Shopify stores: the most common AI-shopping data gaps"
- "Which retail categories have the best machine-readable product data?"
- "The product fields most often missing from ecommerce structured data"

Every report links back to the free scanner.

## Conversion path

```text
Useful public finding
      ↓
Free store scan
      ↓
Connect Shopify
      ↓
See identifiable AI visits/orders/revenue
      ↓
Receive prioritized readiness fixes
      ↓
Paid monitoring / optimization (after validation)
```

## Pricing experiment — do not activate yet

Validate willingness to pay before building billing complexity.

A sensible future test is:

- Free — readiness scan + limited recent attribution
- Pro — ongoing attribution, history, product-level insights, alerts
- Growth — multi-store, benchmarks, exports/API, deeper optimization

Prices should be tested with real users rather than hard-coded before demand exists.

## Success metrics for validation

The first milestones are not vanity signups.

Track:

1. scanner completion rate
2. scan → Shopify connection rate
3. number of stores generating real events
4. percentage of stores with identifiable AI referrals
5. merchants returning to dashboard
6. merchants asking for a specific improvement/paid feature
7. first merchant willing to pay

## Credibility rule

AgentCart should be conservative about attribution. If the source cannot be identified, say `Direct / unknown`. The long-term brand advantage is being the analytics product that does not invent AI-attributed revenue.
